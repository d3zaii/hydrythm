Technology

A 4-Stage Filtration System

Polluted air enters, passes through a water-film chamber, and exits as a personal clean-air bubble — all in a unit lighter than 500 grams.

01
💨
Side Intake Grills
Polluted air enters from both sides, away from your face, through protective grills with a coarse pre-filter mesh for large particles.
02
🌀
Microfan-Assisted Flow
Two quiet centrifugal micro-blowers pull air at 20–40 L/min with adjustable low, medium, and high modes for comfort vs. protection.
03
💧
Water Filtration Chamber
Air passes through a transparent 30–50 ml water cartridge. Microdroplets and water films trap PM2.5, PM10, dust, pollen, and gases — like industrial wet scrubbers, miniaturized.
04
✨
Clean Air Delivery
Purified air is gently blown into a soft face-seal zone around your nose and mouth, creating a personal clean-air bubble. Excess air vents downward.

Build It Yourself

Complete Materials List

Every component is available off-the-shelf. All buildable with a 3D printer, soldering iron, and parts from Amazon, Flipkart, or IndiaMART.

3D Printed
Main Helmet Shell + Intake Grills
PETG filament 1.75 mm, black or matte finish for durability and UV resistance — approx. 350–450 g filament per unit.
Flexible
Face-Seal Padding Frame
Soft TPU filament (flexible) or 5 mm thick EVA foam sheet, cut to shape for comfortable all-day wear.
Hardware
Adjustable Head Straps
25 mm nylon webbing (2 m length) + plastic buckles + Velcro for a universal fit.
Comfort
Interior Padding
10 mm thick closed-cell foam or memory foam scraps glued inside shell for comfort during extended wear.
Clear PETG
Transparent Cartridge Body
Clear PETG filament or 3–5 mm acrylic sheet — must be fully see-through so you watch dirty water collect in real time.
Filtration Media
Internal Wicks & Mesh
Non-woven polypropylene fabric or thin open-cell foam, 2–3 layers, creates microdroplets and water film to trap particles.
Sealing
Silicone Gasket / O-Ring
Food-grade silicone sheet or O-ring cord. Prevents leaks. 4× small plastic or metal clips for quick-release latches.
3D Printed
Baffles & Droplet Separators
PETG printed internals prevent sloshing and water droplet carry-over into the clean air stream.
Core Component
5V Centrifugal Micro-Blowers × 2
30–40 mm high-static-pressure blowers, 10–15 CFM each. Similar to CPAP mask fans. ~₹250–450 each on Amazon.in or IndiaMART.
Power
LiPo Battery Pack
3.7V or 7.4V LiPo 2000–3000 mAh with built-in BMS + USB-C charging board. Or repurposed power-bank internals. ~₹400–800.
Control
Speed Controller
5V buck converter + potentiometer or Arduino Nano for 3-speed control. ₹150–300. Waterproof toggle/push-button with LED indicator.
Pre-Filter
Washable Intake Mesh
Spun-bond polypropylene or fine stainless-steel mesh cut to 57–60 mm diameter circles. Reusable and washable.
Wiring
22 AWG Silicone Wire & Connectors
JST or XT30 connectors + heat-shrink tubing. Basic PCB or perfboard for fan + battery connections.
Optional V2
PM2.5 Sensor Module
~₹300. Pairs with a small OLED display to show real-time AQI and battery percentage.
Waterproofing
Epoxy + Silicone Sealant
Seal all joints and seams. Hot glue for structural bonds. Critical for protecting the electronics from the water cartridge.
Fasteners
M3/M4 Stainless Screws
Heat-set inserts for 3D prints. Soldering iron + glue gun + multimeter needed for assembly. Build time: 10–15h printing, 4–6h assembly.
The Vision

Clean Air Shouldn't Be a Luxury

📍 Designed for the world · Scaling from Dubai, UAE

Hydrythm was born from frustration with Delhi-level AQI and a belief that clean air is a right, not a privilege. After 8 months of research, the blueprint is ready. The first prototype is buildable today — and the path to mass manufacturing is mapped.

①
Prototype
Build and test the first working unit. Validate 70–85% PM2.5 filtration in real conditions.
②
Iterate
Refine the cartridge, improve efficiency, add PM2.5 sensor, optimize battery life to 10+ hours.
③
Scale
Move to injection-molded ABS shell + mass-produced cartridge. Retail target: under $40–70.
